Provenance Ledger

The analysis page guesses whether a picture is AI-made. This page proves where a picture came from, if its creator wrote it down here first. Registering writes a signed record of the file's fingerprints into a block; blocks are chained together by their hashes, so nobody can change an old record without it showing. Checking a file looks it up by those fingerprints and answers with one of four verdicts — and only when nothing is found does it fall back to the AI detector, clearly marked as a guess.

Corrections are new records, never edits

Nothing on the ledger is ever changed or deleted. A creator can revoke their own registration, anyone can dispute one, and Deep-Guard writes down its resolution. Each of these is a new signed record in a new block, pointing at the record it is about. Record IDs are shown in every result and in the explorer.
